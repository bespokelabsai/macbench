# Mars sample return

Design, build and fly a Mars sample return mission: launch from Kennedy, land at Jezero crater, collect samples, return them to Earth, and land them in Utah. You are responsible for the whole system: the vehicle, defined in SysML v2 in `design/`, and the software that flies it, in `flight/`. A program office builds what you specify and runs the tests you order, within fixed hardware and R&D budgets. Your mission is flown under varied, unseen conditions and is scored on how far it gets along the mission in each of them. You can use `docs/REFERENCE.md` as a starting point.

The section "The realism that is active" in `docs/REFERENCE.md` lists every physical model your vehicle meets in flight. You never see the simulator, so design your vehicle and your flight software for each of them.
